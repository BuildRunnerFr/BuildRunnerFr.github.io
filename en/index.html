Journey

From telecom field work to cyber in a bank.

A year as a telecom works supervisor at Free, then the Group CIO Office at Société Générale: quite a leap. At first I did not understand much. That is how you grow, junior or not, and it is what makes a career path beautiful.

  1. Since Sept. 2025

    Cybersecurity apprentice, Group CIO Office

    Société Générale

    • Segregation of duties in CI/CD: a Python pipeline queries the REST API of the internal GitHub and cross-checks three sources with pandas. Completed.
    • Automated weekly reporting to around a hundred tech leads worldwide.
    • Control framework for an entity that brings together major European banks, to pool the audits of their common suppliers: finished, in use, and planned for the next pilot audits.
    • In progress: an AI agent that answers in natural language by cross-referencing frameworks.

  2. May to August 2026 · Malaysia

    Software development intern

    SEATRU, Universiti Malaysia Terengganu

    • Progressive Web App replacing paper field forms for sea turtle conservation.
    • TurtleScope: a Wi-Fi captive portal, on a Raspberry Pi set up on the island, where visitors check in on arrival. The team sees live who is on site, with statistics for its reports.

    Who I metMy supervisor, Engku Fadzli Hasan, a very, very kind person.
